# Oops! All Juhani

### Scene: The Grove — Dantooine

*A Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic dialogue and encounter mod*

**Premise:** Revan encounters Juhani in the grove. An opportunity to resolve the confrontation peacefully presents itself, but Revan's arrogance provokes an already emotionally unstable Jedi into unleashing her power on the party.

The result is absurd. Nobody involved finds it particularly funny.

## Scene 4 — Combat

**Encounter: Three Juhanis**

All three opponents are hostile.

Carth and Mission retain their identities internally, but their appearance and combat capabilities have been replaced.

The player must defeat all three opponents.

Upon defeat:

- The two transformed companions collapse.
- Their original appearances are restored.
- Both remain unconscious and cannot rejoin the party until revived through dialogue.
- Juhani falls to her knees, exhausted and defeated.

**[COMBAT ENDS]**

## Implementation notes

I'd treat this as **one special encounter with three dialogue sequences**, not a separate quest.

| Component | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Initial conversation | New insult branch; vanilla encounter remains accessible |
| Transformation cutscene | Horror animation (no stasis) → Rakghoul transformation VFX, Carth then Mission |
| Combat | Three Juhanis, one fight |
| Juhani's defeat | Modified resolution dialogue |
| Mission's recovery | Short linear dialogue |
| Carth's recovery | Branching conversation with shared emotional beats |
| Mission and Carth's banter | Party banter that starts by itself once both have recovered, before the party leaves the grove |
| Persistent callback | Optional Carth dialogue unlocked afterward |

One important mechanical detail: if the player recovers Carth before Mission, her conversation ends at "I'll be okay. Trust me.", without "But Carth…?" and "Maybe you should go check on him…", so she doesn't suggest checking on someone who's already standing beside her.

And for the narrative, I'd preserve an especially important distinction: **Carth remembers everything. Mission doesn't.**

Mission walks away with a headache and a vague sense that something horrible happened. Carth walks away with the memory of being trapped in someone else's body, unable to control his actions.

That makes his reaction more than mere embarrassment.

---

### One final creative recommendation

Of everything we've written, I think **Scene 3 is the emotional centerpiece**.

Specifically:

**JUHANI:**  
"My master…?"

"She…"

*[Silence]*

"I warned you to leave."

That is the moment when her shame transforms into resentment. Her later outburst should feel like the inevitable consequence of an internal struggle she has already lost.

And the other performance I'd spend time on is Carth's:

> "Something about… having your own mind turned against you…"

It ties the whole ridiculous encounter to the central theme of KOTOR without ever acknowledging the joke.

**The comedy should happen in the mechanics. The characters should experience it as drama.**

That's the principle I'd use to guide the entire mod.

One last thing: I've written the new confrontation and resolution as a self-contained alternative, but since you're modifying the original game, **I'd eventually merge Juhani's defeat back into as much of the vanilla redemption dialogue as possible.** The less you change there, the less work you'll create for yourself—and you've already got the two scenes that matter most: her breaking point and the companions' recovery.

And that's *Oops! All Juhani*. A fully voiced, emotionally grounded, branching RPG encounter about the terrible consequences of being a smartass.

What hath we wrought, indeed. 😂
